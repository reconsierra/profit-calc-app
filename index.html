How to explain this estimate

This is a discussion starter, not a promise. It estimates the potential return from items a workshop may already use or sell occasionally, when they are applied and charged consistently.

Workshop supplies charge: This is a fair-share recovery for keeping incidental stock on hand, such as small parts, everyday consumables and protective materials. It is not tied to one product.

  • Use the workshop's own service volume and product-use rates.
  • Adjust variable item rates so the scenario remains credible.
  • Use the results to identify practical next steps, not to claim guaranteed results.
